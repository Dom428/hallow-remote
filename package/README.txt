HALLOW REMOTE - your phone as a remote for the Hallow apps

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK
2. Right-click the zip > Extract All
3. Double-click INSTALL.bat (no admin needed)
4. Scan the code it shows with your phone's camera, then "Add to Home screen".
   If Windows asks whether Hallow Remote can use the network, allow private networks.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2
- Hallow Clips: save a clip and watch your newest clips on the phone.
- Hallow Vault: Back up now, and the last backup of each game.
- Hallow Overlay: show / hide.
- Sleep, shut down and lock your PC (tap twice to confirm).

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.1
- The QR code uses the pattern phone cameras read most easily (checked against the QR standard).
- Home Wi-Fi only picks your real home-network adapter, never a VPN, WSL or Hyper-V one.

ON YOUR PHONE
- Hallow Mixer: a slider and mute for every channel, mic mute, presets, play/pause and skip
- Hallow Hub: your recent games with their covers - tap one to start it on your PC
- Hallow Toolkit: game session on / off
- Hallow Clips: save a clip, watch your newest clips on the phone
- Hallow Vault: back up now, last backup per game
- Hallow Overlay: show / hide
- This PC: sleep, shut down, lock (tap twice to confirm)
- Your PC: CPU, GPU, GPU temperature, RAM

REACHING YOUR PC
- Anywhere (Tailscale): works wherever your phone has Tailscale switched on.
- Home Wi-Fi only: your phone has to be on the same network.
Only someone with the link can use it. "New link" makes a new one and the old one stops working.
