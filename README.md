# Hallow Remote

Your phone as a remote for the Hallow apps - [Hallow Mixer](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-mixer), [Hallow Hub](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-hub), [Hallow Clips](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-clips), [Hallow Vault](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-vault), [Hallow Overlay](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-overlay) and [Hallow Toolkit](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-toolkit).

- **Mixer:** channel sliders and mutes, mic mute, presets, play / pause / skip
- **Hub:** your recent games with covers - tap to start one on the PC (through Hub, so its preset and game session apply)
- **Toolkit:** game session on / off
- **Clips:** save a clip, your newest clips - watch them on the phone
- **Vault:** back up now, last backup per game
- **Overlay:** show / hide
- **Power:** sleep, shut down or lock the PC (tap twice to confirm)
- **PC:** CPU, GPU, GPU temperature, RAM - and a Live stats page (with FPS, ping and server from Hallow Overlay when it shares them)
- **My buttons:** your own grid of the actions above
- **Media and apps:** PC volume and mute, what's playing and play / pause / skip (any player), and apps to open from a list you set up on the PC
- **Clipboard and screen peek:** send text to the PC clipboard; take one picture of the screen when you ask (both opt-in)
- **Files:** send files from the phone into `Downloads\Hallow Remote` (opt-in, size cap), and get files the PC puts on its Send to phone list (24 hours)
- **Keys:** your Hallow Keys vault on the phone (HTTPS only; decrypted on the phone) - read-only, or a full editor with Hallow Keys 2.7's "Let the phone edit" (changes are sealed on the phone; Remote can't read them); one-time share links for single items
- **Home:** a dashboard of big cards - the game you're playing, mic, now playing, your last clip, Hallow Guard's safety score with Quick scan, remote help and saves - with notices at the top when something happens; swipe between tabs
- **Guard and Assist:** [Hallow Guard](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-guard)'s score, last scan and threats, plus a quick scan; [Hallow Assist](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-assist)'s remote help state with Disconnect
- **The newer apps (1.18):** an **Apps** tab with a card for each one you have installed - [Hallow VPN](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-vpn) (connected, server, protocol, kill switch, leak check; Connect fastest / Best for gaming / Disconnect over IKEv2), [Hallow Shield](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-shield) (blocked today), [Hallow Browser](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-browser) (tabs, Shield), [Hallow Home](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-home) (Home Assistant, cameras, feeder, fountain, alerts; Feed now with a confirm step), [Hallow Tasks](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-tasks) (CPU / RAM / GPU), [Hallow Gear](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-gear) (battery per device), [Hallow Email](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-email) (unread count only) and [Hallow Financials](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-financials) (locked or not; Lock now) - and a **Torrents** tab for [Hallow Torrents](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-torrents)
- **The newer apps' newer data (1.20):** Hallow VPN 1.2.6 says itself which buttons would make Windows ask (they're hidden, with the reason) and whether Windows has your Nord login; phone connects are one-off (`--keep-target`), so your own server stays picked on the PC. Your real Hallow Torrents categories in the add-magnet picker and a search of your own per phone. Hallow Gear: "last known · 12 min ago", "waiting for its battery report (game running)" and **Refresh**. Hallow Tasks' fast `quick` numbers, busy apps and Not responding. Hallow Home's own Feed now range and Demo mode. Hallow Email unread per account and why one needs attention. "Spotify ad muted" and ads muted today from Hallow Mixer 3.27
- **Authenticator (1.19):** hosts [Hallow Authenticator](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-authenticator), the family's 2FA app for your phone - your codes live on the phone (encrypted), it works offline once installed, and Remote only keeps encrypted backups it can't open
- **Mouse:** trackpad, clicks, scrolling, typing and arrow keys (also a presentation clicker) - off until you turn it on in the PC window

The PC side is a small tray app that serves a phone web page (add it to your home screen) on your Tailscale or home-network address. Every request needs the secret code in the link; the phone can only do a fixed list of things (and only play clips Hallow Clips lists), and secrets on the PC (like app keys or a Discord link) are never sent. Scan the QR code it shows - it's made on the PC, never by an online service.

**HTTPS (optional):** turn on HTTPS Certificates in your Tailscale admin console (DNS page), then run the `tailscale cert` command the window shows; it writes `<pc>.<tailnet>.ts.net.crt` / `.key` into `%APPDATA%\HallowRemote\tls` and Hallow Remote switches to an https link within a minute. Without them it stays plain HTTP (still only inside your tailnet / home network).

## What's new

- **1.20** - **The newer apps' newer data.** **Hallow VPN 1.2.6+:** the card reads `nextProtocol`, `needsUac`, `target` and `windowsLogin` from its status.json (no more pipe question), shows **Your server** and **Windows login: Saved / Needed**, offers Connect / Disconnect only when Windows won't ask (UAC) and says why otherwise (NordLynx, or no saved Nord login yet - connect once from the PC), and connects with `connect fastest|gaming --keep-target`, so a connect from the phone never changes what Connect means on the PC. **Hallow Torrents 1.8+:** the add-magnet picker lists your own categories (`categories`, names only), and every search and add from a phone carries that page's own id (`--client remote:<id>`, random per browser session), so another phone or Hub can't replace its results. **Hallow Gear 1.3.1+:** remembered batteries read "last known · 12 min ago", a paired device with nothing heard yet during a game reads "waiting for its battery report (game running)", the held low-battery alert shows its device name, and a **Refresh** button (Gear's `refresh`: at most once every 30 s - the phone says when to try again - and never during a game). **Hallow Tasks 1.2+:** live numbers from `quick` (a cached sample at most 5 s old, asked at most every 2.5 s), busy apps (using 5% or more) and how many apps are Not responding - counts only. **Hallow Home 1.1+:** Feed now offers exactly Home's own range (`minPortions` - `maxPortions`, with grams) and the PC checks it again; Demo mode is marked **Demo**. **Hallow Email 1.1:** unread per account - its label only when you chose it and it isn't an email address, otherwise "Account 2" - and why an account needs attention, in plain words (our own words if the text holds an address). **Hallow Mixer 3.27:** "Spotify ad muted" and "3 ads muted today" under the Mixer. Every new value is checked on the PC first, nothing new is logged, and Refresh is a POST with the link's code, the CSRF token, the same origin and a rate limit, like the other actions. Older versions of each app work as before.
- **1.19** - **Hallow Authenticator.** Remote now delivers [Hallow Authenticator](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-authenticator) 1.0, a replacement for Microsoft Authenticator that lives on your phone: open **Home > Authenticator** (HTTPS needed), install it, and your 2FA codes are kept on the phone, encrypted with your own passcode (fingerprint unlock on Android Chrome / iOS 18+), working with the PC off. Remote serves its files from `/<code>/auth/` with a strict Content-Security-Policy (no inline script or style), receives **encrypted backups** the phone sends (Remote can't open them; checked for shape, up to 2 MB, one per 10 s) into `%APPDATA%HallowAuthenticatorackups` for you to copy to a NAS, lists them for a restore, carries **Hallow Keys 2.11's one-time transfer** of your 2FA keys (sealed end to end with a code shown on the PC; kept in memory, given out once within 10 minutes) and, only if you turn it on, the **PC mirror** for Hallow Keys (sealed, `mirror.bin`). All of it needs the link's code, the page's CSRF token and the same origin, and is rate-limited. The app's files are a copy of the Authenticator release embedded in the exe (`src/web/auth`). **A compact PC window:** smaller (600 x 640, fits a 1366 x 768 screen; resizable, and the size you give it is remembered), with the essentials on top - the code and link, a **Remote on** switch and whether your phone is connected - and the rest in sections you open when you need them: Phone permissions, Apps your phone can open, Send files to your phone, Advanced (tray, start with Windows, Hallow icon, accent, look, updates) and Help. The long HTTPS steps are one line with a **How** button. Nothing was removed.
- **1.18** - **Your phone controls the newer apps.** A new **Apps** tab groups a card for each newer Hallow app you have installed (Network and privacy, Home, This PC, Personal): **Hallow VPN** - connected or not, server name, country and protocol, kill switch, leak check, with **Connect fastest**, **Best for gaming** and **Disconnect** when the connection is IKEv2 (NordLynx needs a Windows prompt on the PC, so the card says to use the PC); **Hallow Shield** blocked today; **Hallow Browser** tabs and Shield; **Hallow Home** - Home Assistant, cameras online, feeder, fountain, the door lock's state (shown only - nothing can unlock it) and alerts, with **Feed now** (pick 1-12 portions, then confirm); **Hallow Tasks** CPU / RAM / GPU; **Hallow Gear** battery, charging and asleep per device; **Hallow Email** the unread count only; **Hallow Financials** locked or unlocked, with **Lock now**. A new **Torrents** tab for **Hallow Torrents** (1.4+): every torrent with progress, speeds, state and the VPN lock, overall stats, pause / resume one or all, add a magnet link (with a category), and search your own indexers and add a result. Each app is read only through its documented pipe commands and status.json, and only a fixed list of fields reaches the phone - never keys, tokens, addresses, account names, e-mail subjects or senders, or amounts; torrent names are shown only on the Torrents tab and never logged. **Safer actions:** the new actions are POSTs that need the link's code, a per-run token the page gets from the PC (CSRF), the same origin, and they are rate-limited (Feed now once a minute, VPN every 5 s, 30 actions a minute in all).
- **1.17** - **Light mode readability fixes.** In light mode the PC window's filled accent (Update / OK buttons, the picked chips) follows the Hallow family rule from [Hallow Toolkit](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-toolkit) 5.16: the real accent, unless neither dark nor white text reaches 4.5:1 on it (e.g. violet `#8B5CF6`) - then a slightly darker shade with white text. The accent as text / thin lines is darkened to read on every light surface (4.5:1 on `#EEEAF5`), and off switches use a darker track so the knob shows (as in Hallow Toolkit). The phone page is unchanged.
- **1.16** - **Light and dark mode.** The PC window follows the family setting in [Hallow Toolkit](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-toolkit) (`mode` in `%APPDATA%\Hallow\theme.json`: Light, Dark or Same as Windows - live, no restart), or pick **Look: Follow Hallow / Light / Dark** for Remote alone under the accent switch. The light look uses the Hallow light palette, with the accent darkened for text so it stays readable, and a light title bar. The **phone page** follows the phone's own light / dark setting, or pick **Auto / Light / Dark** at the bottom of Home (kept on the phone only). Hallow Keys share pages follow the browser.
- **1.15** - More of the family on the phone. **Mixer per-app volumes**: under each channel on the Remote tab, every app in [Hallow Mixer](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-mixer) (3.24+) gets its own slider, Mute and **Move** to another channel (only apps Mixer lists can be changed). **Hallow Guard**: the Guard card adds this week's change from the weekly report and Windows hardening, and a new **Family** tab has the whole report (score then / now, fixed, new, still to look at, scans) with **Open report on PC** / **Hardening on PC**. **[Hallow Shield](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-shield)**: ads and trackers blocked today and this week (per browser on the Family tab), read from Shield's status.json (1.6+), or "Shield isn't sharing counts" with how to turn it on. **Hallow Assist**: your own PCs with an online dot and **Open on PC** (Assist 1.10+), which only opens Assist's window with that PC picked - you still click Connect on the PC, and the phone can only pick a PC Assist itself lists.
- **1.14** - New app icon: the Remote phone with signal waves, in the Hallow family style (the exe, the tray / window and the phone's home-screen icons).
- **1.13** - **Home**, a new first tab: big cards for the game you're playing (FPS and ping when Hallow Overlay shares them, Save clip), the mic, now playing with play / pause / skip, your last clip (tap to watch), [Hallow Guard](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-guard)'s safety score with **Quick scan**, **remote help** from [Hallow Assist](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-assist) (who's connected, and **Disconnect** with a second tap - the phone can open Assist's window but can never start a session) and your saves. Swipe left and right between tabs. **Notices** at the top while the page is open: clip saved, backup finished, a damaged backup, a threat found or a scan finished, someone asking to connect - and, if you turn it on, as phone notifications while the page is open in the background (HTTPS; nothing goes through Google's or Apple's push servers). **A real home-screen app**: proper icons (also for Android's shaped icons and iPhones), a splash while it connects, an install button or Add to Home Screen steps, home-screen shortcuts, and it opens without a connection (only the page and icons are kept on the phone). **Safer**: the page may only talk to your PC (Content-Security-Policy), and an address that tries many different wrong links is shut out for 15 minutes.
- **1.12** - For [Hallow Keys](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-keys) 2.7: the **Keys** tab becomes a full editor when "Let the phone edit" is on in Hallow Keys - **+ New** (login, secure note, card, identity), **Edit**, **Trash**, a password **generator**, 2FA keys with the live code as you type, folders and vaults (and filters for both), search. Each change is sealed on the phone with the phone copy's key and posted to `api/keys/change`; Remote only checks its shape and size and hands it to Hallow Keys over the family pipe, then shows Keys' answer (saved, a conflict copy, or why it was refused). **One-time share links**: Hallow Keys can ask Remote to serve one encrypted item (.hkshare) at `https://<your PC>/share/<random id>` - kept in memory only, served once, gone when opened or when it runs out (at most 7 days); the page asks for the passphrase and opens it in the browser (Argon2id in plain JavaScript + WebCrypto). The link has no part of Remote's own code in it. HTTPS only.
- **1.11** - Files both ways. From the phone: a Files tab sends files into `Downloads\Hallow Remote` on the PC - off until you turn it on in the PC window, up to 200 MB by default (50 MB to 4 GB), written straight to disk, never over an existing file (`name (2).ext`), with safe names only (no folders or Windows device names), marked as downloaded from another computer, and the PC tells you each time (through the Hallow icon when Hub hosts it). Pausing the remote stops a file part-way. To the phone: drag files onto the PC window (or Add a file) and they show up on the phone's Files tab for 24 hours - the phone sees names and sizes, never paths.
- **1.10** - A Keys tab for [Hallow Keys](https://github.com/Dom428/hallow-keys): its read-only phone copy, opened on your phone with your master password (decrypted on the phone with WebCrypto - needs HTTPS). The page and the encrypted copy also open without a connection.
- **1.9** - Send text from the phone to the PC clipboard, and Screen peek: one picture of the PC's main screen when you tap. Both are off until you turn them on in the PC window; the PC tells you each time the screen is peeked.
- **1.8** - Media without Hallow Mixer: play / pause / skip with the media keys, what's playing on the PC (Windows' own media info), and the PC's volume and mute. Open an app on the PC from a list you set up in the PC window (the phone only sees the names).
- **1.7** - Updates itself from GitHub (asks first, checks the download's SHA-256). Trackpad and keyboard on the phone's Mouse page - off by default, behind its own switch on the PC, never while the remote is paused.
- **1.6** - Live stats: a big-number page on the phone - FPS, ping and server region when Hallow Overlay shares them (plus its last match), and your PC's CPU, GPU, temperature and RAM. My buttons: build your own grid from the actions Remote already has (choose, order, rename); Sleep / Shut down / Lock still need a second tap.
- **1.5** - The Hallow accent: the colour you pick in Hallow Toolkit becomes Remote's accent on the PC and on your phone, live (setting "Use the Hallow accent", on by default). Crash reports are kept on this PC (`%APPDATA%\HallowRemote\crashes`, newest 20, never your settings or the link's code) with a notice on the next start - nothing is sent anywhere.
- **1.4** - One Hallow icon: when Hallow Hub 2.4+ hosts the family's tray icon, Remote hides its own and lives in Hub's menu (Show the phone code, Copy link, Pause the remote). Setting "Use the Hallow icon" (on by default). The link and its code never go to Hub - Copy link runs inside Remote.
- **1.3** - Live updates: the phone changes the moment something changes on the PC (mic, mixer, clips, backups...), no refreshing. Optional HTTPS with Tailscale's own certificate for your PC - the window shows how to turn it on; the old http link forwards to the new one.
- **1.2** - Hallow Clips (save a clip, watch your newest clips on the phone), Hallow Vault (back up now, last backup per game), Hallow Overlay (show / hide), and sleep / shut down / lock with a confirm tap. Remote joins the Hallow family link (`HallowRemote.exe --cmd ping` / `status`).
- **1.1** - The QR code now uses the pattern (mask) that cameras read most easily, checked against the QR standard; Home Wi-Fi mode picks your real home-network adapter (not a VPN, WSL or Hyper-V one).

Built with the C# compiler that ships with Windows (.NET Framework 4.8): run `build.ps1`. No admin needed.
